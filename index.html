Why this matters for optimization

You can describe a feasible set either by its points or by inequalities aᵀx ≤ β. For closed convex sets, containing closed half-spaces reconstruct the whole set. At an optimum, active constraints are the walls that touch the solution.

A technical distinction worth remembering

A hyperplane is the equality boundary aᵀx = β; a half-space is an inequality side. The support function hC(u) = supx∈C uᵀx records a bounding wall in each direction. This is related to duality but not the same thing as a Lagrangian dual problem.
